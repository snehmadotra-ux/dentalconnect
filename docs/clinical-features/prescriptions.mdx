# Digital Prescriptions

DentalConnect allows licensed practitioners to generate clear, structured digital prescriptions (Rx) tied directly to patient visits.

---

## Structure of a Digital Prescription

A prescription record in DentalConnect consists of:
1. **Prescription Header**:
   - Issuing Dentist's full legal name, board license number, and clinic contact.
   - Patient identity, date of birth, and known allergies (e.g. *Penicillin allergy*).
   - Date and time of issuance.
2. **Itemized Medications (`prescription_items`)**:
   - **Medication Name**: Generic / brand formulation (e.g. `Amoxicillin 500mg (Oral Capsule)`).
   - **Dosage**: Specific unit measure (e.g. `500mg`, `1 tablet`).
   - **Frequency**: Clear timing intervals (e.g. `Three times daily (every 8 hours)`).
   - **Duration**: Total course length (e.g. `7 days`).
   - **Instructions / Precautions**: Clinical guidance (e.g. `Take with full glass of water after food. Complete entire course.`).
3. **Clinical Notes**: Post-operative care guidelines or warnings.

## Common Dental Formulations

The built-in prescription catalog includes standard dental therapeutics:

| Drug Category | Common Medication | Typical Dosage | Clinical Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Antibiotic** | Amoxicillin | 500mg q8h x 7d | Acute odontogenic infection or post-surgical prophylaxis. |
| **Antibiotic (Penicillin Allergy)**| Clindamycin | 300mg q6h x 7d | Alternative for penicillin-allergic patients. |
| **NSAID Analgesic** | Ibuprofen | 600mg q6h PRN | Post-op inflammation and dental pain management. |
| **Antiseptic Rinse** | Chlorhexidine Gluconate 0.12% | 15ml rinse BID x 14d | Periodontal pocket decontamination & dry socket prevention. |

---

## Patient Safety & Allergy Cross-Check

The patient profile displays active medical allergies (such as Penicillin, Latex, or Codeine). When issuing a prescription, the practitioner can reference these alerts directly from the clinical dashboard before submission.
