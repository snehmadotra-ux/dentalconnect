# Welcome to DentalConnect

**DentalConnect** is a specialized, open-source clinical practice and patient engagement platform designed specifically for dental operatories, group clinics, and solo practitioners.

Unlike generic electronic health record (EHR) systems that treat dentistry like general medicine, DentalConnect is architected around the unique workflow of dental medicine: **tooth-by-tooth diagnosis, spatial surfaces (MODBL), procedure ledgers, and digital prescriptions.**

---

## The Vision

Traditional dental practices face significant software fragmentation:
- **Paper charts & clipboards** create data loss and slow down operatories.
- **Legacy desktop EHRs** require expensive local servers, lack mobile access, and charge steep per-seat licenses.
- **Patients** have zero visibility into their treatment plan, teeth conditions, or prescription history.

DentalConnect bridges this gap by providing:
1. A **real-time interactive 32-tooth odontogram** accessible on any tablet or browser.
2. A **self-service patient portal** for online bookings, care plans, and digital prescriptions.
3. A **clinical operatory dashboard** for dentists to manage patients, record ADA/CDT procedures, and review radiographs.
4. An **administrative control panel** for practice analytics, doctor license verification, and team onboarding.


- **Zero ORM Overhead**: Built on direct, audited SQL queries via Python's native `psycopg2` driver. No heavy ORM abstraction layers or unexpected connection pooling bugs.
- **Type-Safe Full-Stack**: End-to-end typing from PostgreSQL schemas to Pydantic v2 validation models, and TypeScript interfaces in React/Next.js 14.
- **Mobile-Ready REST API**: Decoupled architecture allowing future native iOS, Android, or tablet operatory apps to plug in seamlessly.
